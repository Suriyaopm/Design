Welcome to Marudham

A college where things grow

Founded in 1921 by the Madras Mission Council, Marudham is a Christian minority, autonomous college of 7,800 students. Its name comes from marutham, the fertile river-plain landscape of classical Tamil poetry.

We teach the arts, sciences and commerce in two shifts. Every student joins a hall, a club and an extension service such as NSS or NCC, so learning carries on outside the classroom.

Principal’s message

A college is measured by the habits it leaves in its students: reading closely, questioning kindly, and turning up for people who cannot repay you.

For a hundred and five years Marudham has taught first-generation learners alongside the children of its own alumni. This year we added four programmes, opened the Centre for Climate Studies, and moved every syllabus to outcome-based learning.

Dr. Priya JebakumarPrincipal & Secretary · M.Sc., Ph.D. (Botany)

Our story

From a mission hall to a 186-acre campus

The college began with 64 students and four teachers in a borrowed hall in Saidapet. In 1926 it moved to the scrub forest at Tambaram, where the founders planted the neem avenue that still shades the main road.

Governance & IQAC

Statutory bodies

As an autonomous college, Marudham frames its own curriculum through Boards of Studies and an Academic Council, under UGC regulations and the University of Madras.

Governing Body

Meets twice a year. Approves budgets, appointments and new programmes. Includes nominees of the University, the State Government and the UGC.

Academic Council

Approves syllabi, regulations and evaluation reforms recommended by the 22 Boards of Studies.

Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)

Coordinates the AQAR, academic audit, feedback analysis and the NIRF and NAAC submissions. Coordinator: Dr. Leela Thomas.

Grievance, Anti-Ragging & ICC

Statutory committees with student representatives, reachable through the Help Desk and in confidence by email.

How study works at Marudham

All programmes follow the Choice Based Credit System with a Learning Outcome-based Curriculum Framework (LOCF), two semesters a year, and continuous internal assessment.

Two shifts

One college, two timetables

Aided programmes run in Shift I and self-financed programmes in Shift II. Both share the same faculty, library, labs and examination system.

Eligibility

Undergraduate

  • Pass in Class XII (HSC) from a recognised board
  • B.Sc. sciences need the relevant subject in Class XII
  • B.Com and BBA need Commerce or Mathematics in Class XII
  • Sports and NCC quota: certificates from district level or above
Eligibility

Postgraduate

  • Bachelor’s degree in the relevant discipline
  • Minimum 50% in Part III (45% for SC/ST)
  • MCA needs Mathematics at Class XII or degree level
  • Ph.D.: entrance test and interview, per UGC 2022 rules
Documents

Bring these to counselling

  • Class X and XII mark sheets
  • Transfer certificate
  • Community certificate
  • Aadhaar card
  • 4 passport-size photographs
  • First-graduate certificate, if any

FAQ

Common questions

Is there an entrance test for UG admission?

No. UG admission is on Class XII marks. Visual Communication has a short aptitude interview.

Can I apply for more than one programme?

Yes. One application covers up to three programmes in order of preference.

Are scholarships available?

The Scholarship Cell processes state and central scholarships. The college also funds 400 merit-cum-means scholarships every year.

Is hostel accommodation guaranteed?

Hostel seats are allotted by distance from home and merit. Apply in the same form.
