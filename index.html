Welcome to St. Mary’s

A college dedicated to Mary, the mother of Jesus

St. Mary’s College of Education and Research Institute was founded in 2008. It is owned and managed by the Congregation of Sisters of St. Ann of Providence, Southern Province, Tamil Nadu.

The college is a self-financing institution affiliated to the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University and recognised by the NCTE. It sits in a prime locality of Chengalpattu, in surroundings well suited to teaching and learning.

College profile

A temple of learning since 2008

The college was established in 2008 as a self-financing institution. It is affiliated to the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University and recognised by the NCTE.

This temple of learning is dedicated to Mary, the mother of Jesus, whose constant assistance and guidance enable us to live in love and service. Her grace is the motivating force to serve society with sincerity, simplicity and love. Besides moulding students, the college instils social responsibility and spiritual awakening true to the Marian motto “Love and Serve”.

College emblem

The coat of arms

The coat of arms symbolises the educational philosophy of this Christian institution.

  • The letter M with a crown stands for Mary, the Queen of the Institute, who guides teachers and students to spread the message of love and service.
  • The lilies on either side symbolise purity and prosperity, and inspire the team to progress with purity of intention.
  • The words Love and Serve carry the Marian motto of the college.

Vision

True to the Marian motto, Love and Serve, we strive to create integrated women leaders, empowered and committed to excellence, pro-active, innovative custodians of culture and heritage, channels of human values and lights of God’s transforming love to serve humanity.

Mission

Our mission is to form teacher-citizens for humanity who are:

  • Intellectually well developed
  • Socially concerned
  • Morally upright
  • Spiritually oriented

Objectives

  • Equip teachers to develop and practise innovative methods and strategies in teaching and learning
  • Promote learning, research, extension activities and development
  • Build effective communication skills and leadership qualities
  • Encourage students through versatile activities
  • Grow into a centre of excellence in teacher education for preparing quality teachers

Core values

The values which guide us:

  • Trust in God
  • Moral uprightness
  • Social responsibility
  • Pursuit of excellence

    Who can apply

    Graduates with at least 50% marks in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Science, Social Science or Humanities (55% for Engineering graduates), with a 10+2+3 pattern of study. Lower minimums apply for reserved categories.
